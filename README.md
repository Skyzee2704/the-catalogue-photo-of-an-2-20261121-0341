"The Kettle That Pours Light" — a catalogue photograph of an impossible
gadget for the make.fun bounty
[The Catalogue Photo of an Impossible Gadget](https://make.fun/bounty/the-catalogue-photo-of-an-2-20261121-0341).

A polished stainless steel electric kettle, tilted mid-pour on a clean
white studio background — but what comes out of its spout is not water:
a thick stream of glowing golden light, pouring into a small white
ceramic cup below, pooling and glowing inside it. One accessory only:
the cup. Studio light, soft shadows, believable brushed metal.

`main.py` (standard library only) copies `entry.png` (1024x1024, PNG)
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
